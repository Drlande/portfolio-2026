About

About Dax Landess

I'm Dax, a Creative Technologies student at Illinois State University studying game design.

I'm most interested in level design, environmental storytelling and illustration. On my capstone I'm the producer, game designer and art director, so I handle planning, gameplay ideas and the look of the game.

I've designed two board games and done freelance drawing commissions. In summer 2026 I studied Japanese at Nanzan University in Nagoya, and I'm still taking Japanese with a minor in East Asian Studies. I graduate in 2027.
