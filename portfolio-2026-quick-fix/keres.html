Work / Keres

Keres

Scripting project. A top-down wave survival game I made in Unity for my scripting class.

Class
CTK 353, spring 2026
My role
Design and programming
Engine
Unity 6, C#

01

How it plays

  1. Survive the wave.
  2. Earn Souls.
  3. Spend them in the shop, then start the next wave when you're ready.
  4. Fight the boss, the Warden of Erebus.
  5. When you die, you get your stats.

02

HUD

Everything is placeholder shapes, so the HUD does the work: health, wave, kills, Souls, score, time and your current weapon.

03

Process

In April it was a movement and mouse-aim test. By May it was the full arena game. Later I ported my capstone's pixel filter to it as a URP shader.
