Work / The North

The North

Tabletop design study. Two factions on a frozen map. First player to earn 1,000 gold and buy a boat wins.

Type
Class project, fall 2025
My role
Map, rules, classes, economy
Rules
Rules and design doc (PDF, 2.5 MB)

01

How it plays

Pick the Vikings or the Nobles and a class. On your turn you can hunt, train, trade at the market or invade the other side. Everything uses a d20. Moving takes a 10 or higher, or you lose the turn.

Vikings are stronger in the wild. Nobles are better at trading.

02

Example turn

  1. A Viking Hunter wants to hunt in the Mountains. She rolls a 7 and loses the turn.
  2. Next turn she rolls a 13 and gets there.
  3. She hunts a Jugg (needs 6+). She rolls a 4, doubled to 8, and wins 25 gold.
