Work / Capstone

Capstone

A first-person exploration game set in an underground religious town. No combat. The game is about the choices you make and how you see the town.

Status
In progress, fall 2026
My role
Producer, game designer, art director
Team
Six students
Engine
Unreal Engine 5.8

01

Overview

You play a young woman who grew up in the town and is about to go through its coming-of-age ritual. The first part of the game makes the town feel like home: tending the mushroom fields, sharing meals, helping people she grew up with. Then things start to feel wrong.

You get further by learning the town and its people, not by getting stronger.

02

Design goals

  1. Getting caught, not dying.If you're caught, you're taken somewhere in the town and have to get back out.
  2. Choices are actions.Drink or refuse, keep or break a promise, go where you're not allowed.
  3. No good vs. evil.Both options should protect something the player cares about.
  4. 3D world, 2D layer.Hold right mouse (the Sight) to see the town the way the community sees it.
  5. Promises are mechanics.A vow changes what you're able to do.

03

What's working

  • The Sight. Holding right mouse fades in a camera filter. The drawn 2D layer is still planned.
  • Style setting. Crisp, Retro pixel or Off, from one post-process material with dithering.

04

Fixes and changes

Checkerboard. At 12 color levels, Retro turned flat walls into a checkerboard. We moved it to 16. Now we test filters on flat surfaces with the camera moving.

Camera. My first concept used a three-quarter view. We switched to first person.

05

My role

  • Producer. I set priorities and the schedule for the team.
  • Game design. I wrote the development, mechanics and audio parts of our design doc.
  • Art direction. Dark and quiet, lit by glowing roots and lanterns. I also presented our filter work to my class.

Where it's at: the chapel area has a working baseline. The rest of the cavern, character models and animation are in progress. Next up are the 2D Sight layer, a daily schedule for NPCs, and getting caught.

06

Credits

Team of six students. Classmates handle programming, writing and 3D art. Some animations are placeholders from outside libraries.
